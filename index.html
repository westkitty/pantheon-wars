    How to play

    You are a god. Win worshippers among mortal towns before the age ends on turn 45. Each turn: act, then press End Turn.

    • Explore — Read Omens lifts the fog near the known world; a Dream Vision reveals the nearest hidden town.
    • Expand — Raise a Shrine where you have followers, a priest or territory. Shrines convert nearby mortals every turn. Ordain Priests (Emissary, Seer or Warden) and send them to preach.
    • Exploit — followers produce Faith; your four domains add bonuses. Keep towns fed and safe: hungry towns do not grow and abandon you.
    • Exterminate — convert rival followers, cast miracles and Eclipse rival shrines. A struck god may answer with a counter-rite.
    • Promise and bargain — swear covenants to towns, send envoys (✉), consecrate pilgrim roads, claim holy places, give shrines a calling.

    Victory: Ascendancy (60% of all mortals, from turn 12), Dominion (every rival forgotten) or Twilight Judgment (highest devotion when turn 45 ends).

    Controls: click a place to inspect it · click your priest's place twice to command it · · drag / arrows / WASD to pan · wheel, pinch or +/− to zoom. Keys can be changed in Settings.
