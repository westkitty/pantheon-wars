    How to play

    You are a god. Win worshippers among mortal settlements before the age ends on turn 45.

    • Explore — Read Omens lifts the fog near the known world; a Dream Vision reveals the nearest hidden settlement.
    • Expand — Raise a Shrine where you have followers, a priest or territory. Shrines convert nearby mortals every turn. Ordain Priests at shrines and send them to preach.
    • Exploit — followers produce Faith; your four domains add bonuses. Winning a settlement (half its people) earns Favor.
    • Exterminate — convert rival followers, cast miracles, and Eclipse rival shrines (each eclipse removes 1 Ward; at 0 the shrine falls).

    Victory: Ascendancy (60% of all mortals follow you, from turn 12), Dominion (every rival forgotten) or Twilight Judgment (highest devotion score when turn 45 ends).

    Controls: click a place to inspect it · click your priest, then a destination · 1–9 powers · Enter end turn · Esc cancel · drag / arrows / WASD to pan · wheel, pinch or +/− to zoom · L log · H help.
