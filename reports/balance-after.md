## Verdict against the success criterion

- **Overall faction win rate, before → after:** spread **53.0 pp → 10.5 pp** (Egypt 67.0 / Greek 34.4 / Norse 14.0 → Egypt 43.2 / Norse 39.5 / Greek 32.7). This is within the 15 pp target. A 3000-game confirmation on a different seed block measured 9.9 pp (`balance-after-3000.md`).
- **Not met, per mode:** the three-god FFA spread is 16.3 pp here (19.6 pp in the 3000-game run). Greek is weak in FFA (about 21–24%) and strong in duels (about 53–59%). The tool's strict verdict line therefore says FAIL, because it checks every mode. It is a consistent structural asymmetry across both seed blocks. One untested hypothesis: the Greek kit (coastal pressure, Aegis wards) pays off in one-on-one races and less in three-way scrums. It is backlog item #2.
- **Seat order:** in duels the first seat won 60.3% of these 1000 games and 53.5% in the 3000-game run. Backlog item #3 covers seat compensation.
