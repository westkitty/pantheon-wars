# Forty-power performance audit — findings

Tool: `tools/perf-powers.js` (real casts; see its header). Report: `reports/perf-powers.md` (generated by `tools/perf-powers-report.js`).
Raw: `reports/perf-powers/{head,2b240d2,34e542d}-run{1,2,3}.json` and the configuration runs `reports/perf-powers/head-*.json`.
Builds: HEAD (6e3f629 code; the only later commits add the benchmark tools and data), 2b240d2 (v0.4, before the authored manifestations),
34e542d (release-readiness candidate, before all art passes). Equivalence: same seed and AI fixture, same targets (the engine and data are
identical in the three builds, so the fixtures and targets match exactly), same camera (target at zoom 1.9), viewport 1440×900 @1, High
quality, fixed 1/60 s frame step, headless Chromium with software rendering. Comparisons are EQUIVALENT in fixture and method.

## What the old benchmark measured
`tools/perf.js` `miracle-burst` fires `addFx('bolt' | 'topple' | 'reveal')` on six towns. It never plays any of the forty manifestations.
It stays as a CONTROL (`reports/perf-powers/control/*.json`), not as evidence about the forty powers.

## Harness defect found and fixed during the audit
The first version rendered frames back to back without yielding to the browser. Canvas work piled up into ~200 ms flushes that never
occur in play (idle p95 ≈ 200 ms against ≈ 14 ms with yielding). The tool now silences the app's own loop, renders once per measured
frame at 1/60 s and yields one animation frame, matching how `perf.js` times frames in play. Those first numbers were discarded.

## Results (three runs per build)
- Coverage: 40/40 powers cast through `PW.act` in every build; each dispatched its own VFX id; 0 effects alive after the lifecycle.
- At HEAD the manifestations are cheap: over the same scene's idle frames they add a median 0.4 ms (median) and 0.6 ms (p95) per frame;
  anticipation +0.6 / +1.2 ms, manifestation +0.7 / +0.1 ms, aftermath +0.1 / 0.0 ms. The most expensive power adds 3.5 ms p95
  (Return of Telipinu, `meadow_wake`); the worst absolute cast p95 is 21.1 ms (Hammer of Thunder, whose fixture scene is the busiest:
  idle p95 19.2 ms). All under the 33 ms budget.
- Builds: idle p95 (median over powers) 13.7 ms HEAD vs 10.7 ms 2b240d2 vs 11.8 ms 34e542d; incremental cast p95 0.6 / 0.9 / 0.1 ms.
  The cost that grew since 34e542d is the scene itself (terrain features, town life, pilgrims, weather and roads drawn at screen
  resolution), not the forty effects. Canvas calls per frame during a cast rise only 1–7% over idle (fill+stroke+drawImage).

## Optimization experiments
- **Rejected — batching `meadow_wake` paths** (one path for all stems, one per petal colour): extra fills per frame during the cast
  fell from +49 to +6 and strokes from +9 to +0.5, but incremental time did not change measurably (A/B, three alternating runs × three
  casts: median +0.2 → +0.1 ms, p95 +0.6 → +0.7 ms), and batching changes the fade blend where petals overlap. Reverted.
- **Not attempted:** caching or pre-rendering manifestations. Their measured cost (sub-millisecond median) does not justify the memory
  or the loss of live motion.
- **Kept from v0.7:** the closed-form local projection (benefits every screen-space object and airborne effect).
No code optimization is accepted in this pass, so no before/after VFX fidelity atlas was needed: the manifestations are unchanged.

## Control: the generic burst scene (`tools/perf.js`, one run each)
miracle-burst renderer time — HEAD median 8.5 ms, p95 14.3 ms; 34e542d median 6.8 ms, p95 11.0 ms. This synthetic scene measures the
scenery plus generic bolt/topple/reveal effects; it says nothing about the forty manifestations.

## Memory

cast of a power: collection timing, not a trend. A forced-collection check is recorded below. `node tools/memory-check.js` (ten games in
one page) stays flat.
